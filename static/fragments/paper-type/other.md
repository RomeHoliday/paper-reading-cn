# Other or mixed paper

Use the generic analytical spine when no single genre dominates:

1. State the research problem and intended contribution with bounded English
   source plus Chinese explanation.
2. Reconstruct the argument from premises to method, evidence, and conclusion.
3. Select the method/design details needed to understand or reproduce the work.
4. Audit the strongest evidence, its conditions, and what it cannot establish.
5. Explain contributions, boundaries, limitations, and availability.
6. If the paper mixes genres, apply the relevant checks from the closest
   fragment to each section without claiming that the whole paper belongs to
   that genre.

Do not import systems baseline rules, empirical causal rules, or theory proof
requirements where the paper does not make those kinds of claims. Record the
mixed classification in `translation_notes.md`.
